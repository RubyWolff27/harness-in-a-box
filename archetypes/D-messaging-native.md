# Archetype D: messaging-native

**One sentence:** You live mainly in iMessage, WhatsApp or a phone call, not a normal chat website.

Example: Instinct (Watch). Overlay: `archetypes/overlays/instinct.md`.

## Strict rules (week one, non-negotiable)

1. **No email connector and no payment connector.** Calendar read-only only after they understand safe mode.
2. **Drafts you send.** Every outbound message is a draft they copy or approve. Re-send this rule in the weekly review.
3. **Shorter seed.** Standing instructions must fit in one message (under about 1,500 characters). Use the seed in the Instinct overlay; do not paste whole skill texts into the thread.
4. **After disconnect.** Ask what text is still kept and how to clear it; record the answer in `My Harness`.
5. **Safe mode.** Pause every routine you can reach; connectors read-only until they say otherwise. Confirm both out loud.
6. **Messaging out** on this archetype means any iMessage, WhatsApp, email or payment action. Lite routines must lack it.

## Capability defaults (Lite)

| Need | Default on D |
|---|---|
| Fetch | Prefer paste of `AGENTS.md` and `lanes/LITE.md` (or ZIP); keep fetches short |
| Memory home | One note or Doc `My Harness` on their phone |
| Playbooks | Inside `My Harness` under **Playbooks** |
| Routines | Lite prompts + risk contract; phone alarms if no scheduler |
| Self-check 7 | May be N/A (platform) if no scheduler, with prompts and alarms given |
