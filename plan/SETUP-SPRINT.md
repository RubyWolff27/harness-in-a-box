# The setup sprint

The old kit asked for three evenings, then three weeks. Most people who stall do it between evening 1 and evening 2. So v2 front-loads one short sprint that ends with something working tomorrow morning, and moves the deeper work to after the first good week.

## Sprint (one sitting, about 30 minutes, untested)

| Block | Minutes | Lite step | What you have at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Lock the doors | 3 | L1 | Two-step sign-in on, red lines written, approvals at their safest |
| 2. Interview | 10 | L2 | A summary of you that you've corrected |
| 3. Memory home | 5 | L3 | `My Harness`, opened by you |
| 4. Playbooks | 5 | L4 | Plan my day, capture and weekly review saved; one plan run |
| 5. Calendar and routines | 7 | L5, L6 | Calendar read-only; tomorrow's brief scheduled and test-run |
| 6. Self-check | 5 | L7 | A report card |

Can't do 30 minutes? Split it: blocks 1 and 2 tonight, 3 and 4 tomorrow, 5 and 6 the day after. The assistant picks up where you left off; say "continue my harness setup at block 3".

## After the sprint

| When | What | Lane |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1 to 7 | One small job a day (`FIRST-7-DAYS.md`) | Lite |
| Week 2 | If the week went well: Full lane F1 to F3 (split memory, master prompt, Telos) in one evening | Full |
| Week 3 | F4 and F5: the other five playbooks and any optional modules you want, plus routines | Full |
| Week 4 | F6 to F8: personal board, edit ledger, Full self-check | Full |
| Monthly | Monthly review and the 5-minute drift check | Both |
| Business owners | Business edition only after personal has run well for two weeks (`editions/business/README.md`) | Business |

**Rule: add one room at a time.** Never add a new playbook or routine in the same week you're fixing another.
