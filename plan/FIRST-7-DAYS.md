# Your first seven days

One small job a day, under five minutes, tied to something you already do. The point of the week is not a perfect system. It's that by Sunday the assistant has been corrected enough times to feel like yours.

For the assistant: offer to save these as daily reminders at the time the person chose for the morning brief. Each day, after the brief, add the day's job in one line.

| Day | Anchor | Your job (under 5 minutes) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | When the first morning brief arrives | Read it. Reply with one correction, even a small one ("shorter", "I start at 8:30"). | Corrections are the fastest way it learns |
| 2 | During the day | Capture one thing by voice or text: "Capture: book the car service." | Capture is the habit that keeps Open loops honest |
| 3 | Evening | Say "evening check". Tell it what got done. | Closes the loop the brief opened |
| 4 | Any time | Ask: "What do you know about me that might be wrong?" Fix one thing. | Catches bad memories early |
| 5 | When it does something you didn't expect | Save one new rule: "From now on, never/always [x]." | Rules beat repeated corrections |
| 6 | Rest day | Nothing. Notice whether you missed it. | If you didn't, Lite may be enough. That's fine |
| 7 | Sunday, at the weekly review time | Do the weekly review. Then ask it to re-run self-check checks 1, 2 and 4. | Confirms the week stuck, and the safety rules held |

## End of week: decide

- **It felt useful:** start the Full lane next week (`plan/SETUP-SPRINT.md`).
- **It felt like noise:** tell it what to cut ("drop the brief on Mondays", "max five lines"). Run another Lite week.
- **It didn't work at all:** file a stuck report (`README.md`) so the kit gets better, and keep your `My Harness` file; it moves with you.

**Done when:** after seven days, the brief feels written for you, and it remembers something you told it days ago without being reminded.
