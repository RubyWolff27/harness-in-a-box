# Which assistant should I use?

**If you already pay for one of these, use that one.** The kit works on all of them, and there is no single "best" pick. Switching costs more than the differences below.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 against each company's own help pages (links in `core/SOURCES.md`). Prices are in US dollars unless stated; check the current plans page in your country before you pay.

## Three questions

1. **Do you already pay for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok Bot?** Use it. Go to step 2 of `README.md`.
2. **Do you want it to work while your phone is in your pocket** (briefs arriving on time, watching your inbox, finishing jobs overnight)? Pick one marked "Yes" under Always on below.
3. **Do you want to spend nothing to start?** Muse, Gemini and ChatGPT Free have free tiers (with limits). Start with the Lite lane.

## Honest trade-offs

- **Always-on agents can do more, so they can do more wrong.** The kit keeps every send, buy, post and delete behind your yes on every path.
- **Free tiers run out.** Muse, Gemini and ChatGPT Free have usage limits. A full setup reads a lot of text; Lite is designed to fit.
- **Your data goes where the assistant runs.** All of these run in their company's cloud. Read the privacy settings in your path guide's first step. Muse on a Mac can read your Messages and WhatsApp if you allow it: only allow what you'd be comfortable with it reading.
- **Don't run two at once while you're setting up.** Less surface is the point. You can move later; the memory home moves with you.
