# Personal autonomy ladder

**One sentence:** Skills climb shadow → draft → act-with-approval → act-and-report; they drop after a failure, and each rung says which trifecta leg is missing.

## Rungs

| Rung | What it may do | Rule-of-Two shape (which trifecta leg it lacks) |
|---|---|---|
| **Shadow** | Runs on sample or past data; output compared, nothing sent | Lacks messaging out and live writes |
| **Draft** | Produces drafts for the person to send | Lacks messaging out (person sends) |
| **Act with approval** | May act after an explicit yes each time | Lacks unattended external action |
| **Act and report** | May act within a written brief, then report | Still lacks at least one leg named in the skill's risk contract |

## Promotion

Only after: (1) the skill's `evals/` samples pass, and (2) the person says yes to the new rung. Show the rung name and the Rule-of-Two shape before promoting.

## Demotion

After any failure (wrong send, wrong write, ignored approval, failed eval on real work): drop one rung automatically and tell the person. Re-promotion needs evals and a yes again.

## Lite

Lite playbooks stay on **Draft** (or Shadow while testing). Do not promote Lite playbooks to Act-and-report in week one.
