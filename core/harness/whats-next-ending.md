# The "What's next" ending rule

Every report, brief, review or long answer (more than about 300 words) ends with a short section:

```markdown
## What's next
1. [Action] (owner: you | assistant), by [when]
2. ...
3. ...
```

Rules:
- At most three items. If nothing needs doing, write "Nothing needed from you."
- Each item is a concrete action, not a topic. "Book the car service for Friday" beats "Car".
- Say who acts. Anything the assistant will do itself must be within what you've already approved.
- Carry unfinished items into open loops, so they don't vanish with the report.

Why: long reports get read and forgotten. A short action list turns reading into doing.
