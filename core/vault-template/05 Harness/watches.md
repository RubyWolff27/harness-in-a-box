# Watches

Things your assistant checks for you, so you do not have to remember to ask. One row each.

| What | Condition to alert | How often | Who acts | Added |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example: a subscription | 7 days before it renews | daily | you decide | YYYY-MM-DD |
| Example: an item you plan to buy | price drops below your target | daily | you decide | YYYY-MM-DD |

Rules: the assistant alerts and drafts, it never buys, cancels or sends. Remove a watch once it has fired or no longer matters.
