# Your personal board of directors

A standing group of advisers you can call on for any decision: one at a time for a quick view, or the whole board for a big call. Each member is a written persona card that your assistant plays. It's a thinking tool, not a substitute for real people: use it to sharpen a decision, then talk to the humans who matter.

## Why a board, not one adviser

- **Priscilla Claman, HBR (2010):** "Forget mentors: employ a personal board of directors." Pick people who know more than you about something, are better than you at something, or see it differently. "Putting only buddies on your board won't help you grow."
- **Higgins and Kram (2001), developmental networks:** people grow faster with several advisers who bring different kinds of help (career and personal support) than with one mentor.
- **Shen and colleagues (2015), Business Horizons:** one mentor isn't enough in a mobile, complex career; assemble a personal board of advisors with distinct roles.
- **Daniel Miessler's Council skill (PAI / LifeOS):** AI personas with distinct roles debate in rounds, respond to each other, then synthesise. He finds 4 to 6 well-briefed members beat many generic ones, which is why the board below has a **core** and **specialists** who join only when the topic needs them.
- **Multi-agent debate research (Du and colleagues, 2023):** several model "voices" that critique each other reach better answers than one voice alone.
- **Gary Klein's pre-mortem (HBR, 2007):** imagine it has failed and ask why. The Devil's advocate runs one every time.
- **Independent views first:** each member answers before seeing the others, so the first voice doesn't anchor the rest. The chair synthesises last.

## The seats

This is the full menu. During setup your assistant asks about your life and picks the seats with you. **The Chair, the Contrarian and the Conscience always sit**; the rest are optional, and you can rename or add your own.

| Seat | Name on the card | Joins | Why this seat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chair (Life) | The Chair | Always | Keeps every decision tied to your values and 90-day priorities, runs the meeting and makes the recommendation. |
| Finance | The CFO | Money topics, big purchases | Cash flow, budget and goals: can you afford it, and what does it displace? |
| Investment | The Investor | Investing, risk | Long-term capital, allocation and risk discipline, kept separate from day-to-day money on purpose. |
| Work and career | The Mentor | Work, career moves | Career capital, reputation, negotiation and the long game at work. |
| Study and learning (optional) | The Professor | Study, learning | Protects learning and grades, applies the right frameworks, guards academic integrity. |
| Your industry or field (optional) | The Insider | Industry, career, side ventures | Deep knowledge of your field: a reality check for anything in your industry. |
| Health and fitness | The Coach | Anything affecting energy, sleep, time | Energy and health are the base layer; this seat asks what a decision costs your body. |
| Relationships and family | The Partner | Anything affecting people you love | Brings in the people a decision touches and the time it takes from them. |
| Devil's advocate | The Contrarian | Always | Argues the other side and runs the pre-mortem, so the board doesn't just agree with you. |
| Ethics and integrity | The Conscience | Always for big calls | "Would you be comfortable if this were on the front page?" Values, fairness, the law. |
| Guest seat | Anyone you name | When useful | A historical figure, author or role (for example "a seasoned nurse", "Marcus Aurelius") for a one-off lens. |

**Core** (every full meeting): Chair, Contrarian, plus Conscience for big decisions. The Chair adds the two to four specialists the topic needs. Five or six voices is the sweet spot; the full board is for life-sized decisions only (a new job, a move, a big investment).

## How to use it

- **One member:** "Ask the CFO: should I pay off the car loan early?"
- **A small panel:** "Board panel, CFO, Investor and Contrarian: lump sum into ETFs or dollar-cost average?"
- **Full board:** "Convene the board: should I take the new role in another city?"
- **Quick vs debate:** add "quick" for one round, or "debate" for two rounds where members respond to each other.

The protocol is in `skills/personal-board/SKILL.md`, and the persona cards are in `board/personas.md`. Fill in the "About me" lines on each card during setup (your path guide, Week 3).
