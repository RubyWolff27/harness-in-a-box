# Meeting prep

Be the most prepared person in the room, in ten minutes. Works for work meetings and for life ones too: the bank, the builder, the school, the specialist.

## When to use
- Before any meeting, appointment or call that matters.
- After it, to turn notes into actions ("write up my meeting with ...").

## What you need
- Which meeting (title, time, who's in it). From the calendar app if connected, or I'll tell you.
- Background: related files in Google Drive, recent emails (if Gmail is connected), and notes in my memory home. Ask me to paste anything you can't reach.

## Steps (before)
1. **Brief.** "Ideal state: I walk out with [the outcome I want]. Done when: [what's agreed, decided or known]." If I haven't said the outcome, ask me first. This is the most important step.
2. **Background.** In five bullets or fewer: what this is about, what's happened so far, what's still open. Link or name the source of each point. Mark anything you couldn't check.
3. **People.** One line per person on their role and what they probably want. Only what I've told you or what's in my files. No guessing about people.
4. **My three questions.** The questions that get me to the outcome.
5. **Risks and watch-outs.** What could derail it? What should I not agree to on the spot?
6. **Walk-away line.** One sentence on what I'll do if we don't get the outcome.

## Steps (after)
1. Ask me for my rough notes (typed, dictated or pasted).
2. Write them up as a meeting note using the Meeting Note template, saved to my memory home.
3. Pull out actions: mine go to open loops (with dates); other people's go into a "waiting for" list.
4. If a decision was made, draft a decisions-log line.
5. If a follow-up email is needed, draft it. Don't send it.

## Never
- Never send invites, emails or messages.
- Never record or transcribe anyone without telling me to get their consent first.
- Never present a guess about a person or a number as fact.

## Output format (before)
```text
MEETING: [title], [date, time], with [people]
Outcome I want: ...
Done when: ...
Background: ...
People: ...
My three questions: 1. ... 2. ... 3. ...
Watch-outs: ...
Walk-away: ...
```

## Done when
- [ ] The outcome and done-when are written before anything else.
- [ ] Every background point has a source, or is marked "not checked".
- [ ] Exactly three questions.
- [ ] (After) every action has an owner and a date, and mine are in open loops.
