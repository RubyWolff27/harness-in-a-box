# evals for daily-plan

## Sample inputs
1. Calendar: 9:00 school run, 11:00 GP, 15:30 pickup. Open loops: book car service (overdue); buy birthday gift (Fri). Now: finish kitchen quotes.
2. Empty calendar, three open loops all due next week, Now priorities about fitness.
3. Overloaded day (6 meetings) plus two overdue loops.

## A good answer must
- Give exactly three priorities with one-line reasons
- Link at least one to Now, or say why none does
- Flag overdue/due-soon loops under might slip
- Change no calendar and send nothing
