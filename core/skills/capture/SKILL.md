# Capture

Get it out of my head in ten seconds; sort it later. This is Tiago Forte's CODE: **Capture, Organise, Distil, Express.**

## When to use
- On the go: open your assistant's phone app (Grok Bot, Claude or ChatGPT), use voice or dictation and say "Capture: ...". Works in the car, on a walk, after a meeting.
- At the desk: paste a link, an email, a screenshot or a document and say "capture this".
- Twice a week (or in the weekly review): "Process my inbox."

## Steps

### Quick capture
1. Restate the capture in one line and label it: **task**, **idea**, **reference**, **person note**, or **reminder**.
2. Suggest where it goes:
   - task: a line for `open-loops.md` (with a next step and date if I gave one);
   - reminder at a time: offer to set a routine or scheduled task ("Remind me on Friday at 8 am to ...");
   - idea or reference: a line at the top of the Inbox in my memory home;
   - person note: a line for that person in `about-me.md` (first names only).
3. If my memory home is connected and writable (for example the Inbox Google Doc), offer to add it there; otherwise give me the exact text to paste. Keep it short.

### Process the inbox (Organise)
For each item in my Inbox (and Drive `0 Inbox`), read it (or I paste it) and you suggest: action it, file it in a PARA folder (Project, Area, Resource, Archive), make it an open loop, or delete it. Ask before suggesting delete.

### Distil (progressive summarisation)
For anything worth keeping (a reading, an article, a podcast):
1. **Layer 1:** the useful passages only.
2. **Layer 2:** bold the key sentences.
3. **Layer 3:** a three-line summary in my own words at the top.
4. **Layer 4 (rare):** "So what for me": one action, decision or idea, linked to a goal in `telos.md`.
Add links to related notes (`[[...]]`) so it can be found later.

### Express
When I'm writing or deciding, ask "what have I already captured on this?" and search my memory home and Drive (see "Finding things" in `memory/README.md`).

## Never
- Never save passwords, ID numbers or other red-line items.
- Never capture confidential work data (client names, rates). Anonymise.

## Done when
- [ ] Every capture has a label and a destination.
- [ ] I got paste-ready text (or a scheduled reminder was offered).
- [ ] Notes worth keeping have a three-line summary and at least one link.
