## Optional module templates

The study, work, money, investment and daily-interest-brief skills are templates. Your assistant researches and links the right official sources for your country, industry and interests during setup (`AGENTS.md` Step 4), so there are no country-specific links here. A good rule for those links: government, regulator and official-body pages first, then reputable outlets, always with the date checked.

## Things I couldn't verify
- Whether ChatGPT's and Claude's apps reliably open a raw GitHub URL pasted in chat. Both usually can with web search on, but I couldn't test it, hence the ZIP fallback.
- Claude: the list of scheduled-task approval modes, and whether a scheduled task can edit a Google Doc with nobody watching.
- Grok Bot: which Google connectors Marketplace offers on your plan, and the price in your currency.
- Prices in your local currency for any of the three (all three list US dollars or vary by country).

- The maximum number of files in a project's Sources on Plus (the ChatGPT path uses 9 files at first and up to 16 after Week 3). If you hit a limit, combine the optional module skills into one file (`CHATGPT.md` Step 2).

- Whether ChatGPT's Google Drive app reliably reads plain `.md` files (only matters if you keep Obsidian in Drive). Google Docs are supported.
- Whether Notion writes and developer mode work on Plus (OpenAI's pages disagree).
- Whether a scheduled task can read your Google Docs (OpenAI names Gmail, Slack and GitHub as examples).
- Whether ChatGPT's Skills page is available on a personal Plus plan in the web app. OpenAI's help page lists business plans; its Learn docs say standalone skills work in the desktop app and Codex.
- Google Calendar as a ChatGPT app is now named in OpenAI's release notes (28 August and 23 September 2026: multiple Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Contacts accounts). Connect it under Settings > Apps.
- Any way to limit the Google Drive app to a single folder on a personal account. I found none.
- A character limit for Project instructions. OpenAI's pages I read don't state one.
