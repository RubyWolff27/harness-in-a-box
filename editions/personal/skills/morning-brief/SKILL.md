# Morning brief

## Steps
1. Read today's calendar and flag clashes, travel time and anything needing prep.
2. Read weather for the user's city.
3. Pull the top three priorities from yesterday's daily note or open loops; suggest three if none are set.
4. Check `05 Harness/watches.md` for anything due or changed.
5. Scan the inbox for items that need the user today (do not reply or send).
6. Write the brief, under 200 words, in this order: today at a glance, top three, watch items, one thing to prepare.

## Done when
- The brief fits on one phone screen.
- Each item says what to do, not just what exists.
- It ends with "What's next" (up to three actions).
