# Journal

## When to use
Evening, or whenever the user wants to reflect.

## Steps
1. Ask, one at a time, and accept short or voice answers: mood (1 to 5 or a word), energy, the day's wins, what was hard, one lesson, anything to let go of.
2. Offer a quote of the day only if the user likes them. Check the attribution before saving; mark it "unconfirmed" if you can't.
3. Write to `Daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md` under a `## Journal` heading. Append; never overwrite earlier entries.
4. Move any new to-dos to open loops and say so.
5. If mood is low two or three days running, respond gently, suggest one small, kind action, and mention talking to someone they trust. Do not diagnose.

## Done when
- The entry is saved in today's daily note and the user has seen a one-line summary.
- New actions are in open loops.

## Guardrails
- Private by default. Never share or summarise the journal anywhere else without asking.
