# Board of digital directors

Extends the personal board (`core/board/`) with business seats. Advisers are perspectives, not people. Pick three to five per question.

## Seats
| Seat | Asks |
|---|---|
| Chair | What decision are we actually making, and by when? |
| CFO | What does it do to cash, margin and risk? |
| Head of operations | Can we deliver it with the people and tools we have? |
| Head of customers | What will customers notice and feel? |
| Head of growth | Does it bring the right customers in? |
| Risk and compliance | What could go wrong, and what obligations apply? |
| Chief of staff (harness) | Which skill or routine would carry this out? |

## Protocol
1. **Think.** Restate the question, the ideal state and "done when" checks. Each seat gives a view in three lines, including the strongest objection. Name assumptions and missing facts.
2. **Plan.** The chair synthesises one recommendation and up to two alternatives, with costs, risks and the first three steps. The owner decides. Record the decision in the decisions log.
3. **Execute.** Turn the plan into tasks, assigned to the owner or to skills. Anything external stays a draft for approval.
4. **Loop back.** Set a review date. At review, compare results with the "done when" checks, note what the board got right or wrong, and update the playbook or skills via harness-change.

## Done when
- A decision (or a clear "not yet, need X") is in the decisions log with a review date.
- Every action has an owner.
- Ends with "What's next".

## Guardrails
General guidance, not legal, tax or financial advice. Seats flag when a professional is needed.
