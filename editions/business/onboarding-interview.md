# Business onboarding interview (about 15 minutes)

Look before you ask: connect calendar, email and files (with permission) and confirm what they show rather than asking. One question at a time. Save answers as fields with a source (asked, observed or inferred) and a "last confirmed" date.

1. **The business in one breath.** What it does, who pays, roughly how many customers, a typical sale.
2. **A good week.** What's true by Friday? Name one or two things you can count. What would you do with five hours back?
3. **Where work comes in.** Email, text, DMs, phone, website, walk-ins. Which channel is messiest?
4. **What eats your week.** Top three tasks by hours. What gets done late at night?
5. **Tools and money.** Bookings or orders, payments and invoicing, accounts, customer notes, files. Who else touches them?
6. **Boundaries.** What must never happen without your OK? Any data it shouldn't see?
7. **Documents.** How do documents arrive and who enters them? Could you share a set of recent, real examples privately for testing (never stored in the repo)?
8. **Close.** The one thing that would make next week better. Play back the north star measure and the first skill to switch on, and confirm.

Re-ask a field when it passes its review date, a system of record contradicts it, or a skill hits a gap. Ask one question at the point of need, not a new questionnaire.
