# Business onboarding interview (about 15 minutes)

This is the interview the harness walks a new business owner through. It builds a structured business profile that decides which skills to unlock and at what autonomy level.

Look before you ask: connect calendar, email and files (with permission) and confirm what they show rather than asking. One question at a time. Save answers as fields with a source (asked, observed or inferred), confidence level and a "last confirmed" date.

## 1. The business in one breath
- What's the business called, and what does it do, in a sentence?
- Who pays you, and roughly how many customers do you have?
- What's a typical job or sale worth?

## 2. A good week (the north star)
- Picture a good week. What's true by Friday? Try to name one or two things you could count (for example, slots filled, invoices paid, hours not working).
- If you got five hours a week back, what would you do with them?

## 3. Where work comes in
- How do requests, orders and questions reach you (email, text, DMs, phone, website, walk-ins)?
- Which channel causes the most mess?

## 4. What eats your week
- List the three tasks that take the most time each week. Roughly how many hours each?
- What do you keep putting off or doing late at night?
- What's the one thing you keep meaning to systematise?

## 5. Tools and money
- What do you use today for bookings or orders, payments and invoicing, accounts, customer notes, and files? It's fine if the answer is "a notes app" or "my head".
- Who else touches these (a bookkeeper, staff, a partner)?
- Can the harness connect to any of them so it can look instead of asking you? *(Offer to connect here rather than asking more questions.)*

## 6. Boundaries
- What must the harness never do without your OK (for example, message customers, change your books, spend money)?
- Is there any data it shouldn't see?

## 7. Documents and samples for testing
*(Ask near the end. The files are sent after the call, not gathered live.)*
- How do documents reach you today: email, paper, photos (for example on WhatsApp), or something else? Roughly how much comes each way?
- Who handles each type (supplier invoices, delivery notes, customer orders, statements), and who enters them into your accounting system?
- Could you share about 50 recent, real incoming documents across those types? A few awkward ones help too, such as an invoice that's already paid, a reminder or a part delivery.
- If you do stock counts: your last stock count sheet and the accounting system entry it became.
- The approved supplier list (if you have one).
- The item or SKU list from your accounting system (item code, description and unit).
- *(Privacy: say that everything is stored privately, never put in the repo or shared, and used only for testing. Nothing is sent to anyone and nothing is written to your accounting system without your approval.)*

## 8. Close
- Of everything you've said, what's the one thing that would make next week better if it just went away?
- *(Harness plays back the north star measure from beat 2 and the first thing it proposes to take on, for the owner to confirm.)*

## Ongoing micro-interviews

Re-ask a field when it passes its review date, a system of record contradicts it, or a skill hits a gap. Ask one question at the point of need, not a new questionnaire.
